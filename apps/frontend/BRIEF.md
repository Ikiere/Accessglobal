# Product brief — AccessGlobal Market (AGM)

## 1. Product truth

| Field | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Business type and industry | Direct-to-consumer international curated goods marketplace and multi-vendor commerce platform. |
| Primary audience | Discerning shoppers seeking authentic, high-quality international craftsmanship (e-bikes, crafted apparel, bench-made footwear, precision home goods, and artisanal fragrances) without middlemen markups or customs friction. |
| Secondary audience | Independent global manufacturers and workshop owners who value direct relationships with global customers. |
| The single value proposition | Curated goods direct from the world's finest independent workshops, delivered with pre-cleared customs and guaranteed door-to-door transit. |
| The one primary conversion action per view | Add to order and complete checkout. |
| What the product actually does | 1. Curates verified makers across 6 primary aisles. 2. Displays truthful technical specifications and provenance. 3. Consolidates multi-vendor checkout into a single transaction with transparent landed costs. |
| What it replaces | Fragmented drop-shipping sites with undisclosed tariffs, mass-market retail markups, and unreliable grey-market imports. |
| Top three objections a buyer has | 1. "Will I be hit with surprise customs or import tariffs?" 2. "Are the products and materials genuine?" 3. "What happens if the item is damaged or does not fit?" |
| Proof that exists today | All listed items include maker provenance (country of manufacture, workshop name), full material breakdowns, and transparent shipping guarantees. |
| Proof that does NOT exist | No fake celebrity endorsements, no fabricated "10,000+ five-star reviews" or misleading animated count-up counters. |

## 2. Brand voice

| Field | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who is speaking | A knowledgeable, design-conscious curator and logistics partner. |
| Three adjectives that are true | Grounded, precise, authoritative. |
| Three adjectives that are NOT true | Hyped, generic, flashy. |
| Sentence length and vocabulary level | Crisp, confident, clear sentences; specific material and manufacturing terms. |
| Use of "you" / "we" | Focused on "you" (the buyer's experience and choices); "we" only when detailing logistics guarantees. |
| How numbers, dates and currency are presented | Explicit currency symbols (`$`), tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), concrete dimensions and weights (kg, mm, ml, km). |
| Phrases the brand refuses to say | "Revolutionary", "All-in-one", "Disrupting commerce", "Exclusive drop", "Hurry before it's gone". |
| Typography | Display: `Instrument Serif`, serif; Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif; Data/Mono: `IBM Plex Mono`, monospace. |
| Accent color and semantic job | `#124330` (Deep British racing / pine green) for primary actions and verified trust signals. |
